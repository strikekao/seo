🇬🇧 English Privacy Policy

1. 100% Local Data Processing (activeTab & scripting permissions):
Both SnapPDF Pro and Click2Sheet Pro execute all optical screen capturing, canvas stitching, PDF generation, and HTML table extraction strictly locally inside your browser memory on your computer. We do NOT collect, transmit, store, or upload any browsing history, webpage URLs, screenshots, PDFs, or scraped data to any external server.

2. License Verification & Local Storage (storage & host_permissions):
We use chrome.storage.local exclusively on your device to store your Pro activation state and a randomly generated anonymous local identifier (Device_ID). Only when you voluntarily enter your checkout email address and click the Activate / Verify button inside the extension popup does the extension send an HTTPS request containing your email address, Device_ID, and extension identifier to our official Google Apps Script verification endpoint (script.google.com) strictly to validate your Pro license.

3. Zero Third-Party Sharing & Zero Remote Code:
We never sell, rent, trade, or transfer user data to any third parties, nor do we use data for advertising or creditworthiness. All executable code is 100% packaged locally within the extension.

4. Data Deletion & Contact:
To request immediate reset or deletion of your license activation record, email us at strike_kao@hotmail.com.
